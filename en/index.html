A LITTLE MORE ABOUT MY WORLD

Frequently asked questions

Do activities need internet?

Internet is needed to open the website and download the APK. Your last card stays in this browser. Android app cards are stored on the device. For speech, install a voice for the learning language; some voices require internet.

What if there is no sound?

Check media volume and Bluetooth audio output. Add a voice for the selected learning language in your device speech settings. Return to the lesson and press Listen.

Are there ads or purchases?

My World has no ads, in-app purchases or required registration.

How can I report a problem or share an idea?

Email vivelko888@gmail.com. Tell us your device model and what happened. Do not send your child’s personal details.
